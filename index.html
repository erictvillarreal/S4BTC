Key Findings (validated, cross-checked)

  1. A live loss of -0.98% was diagnosed to root cause and reversed. During 30 days of real paper trading (May–Jun 2026), the system lost -0.98% with a 39.66% winrate — far below the historically expected 74.22% for the same regime. A rigorous diagnosis ruled out low volatility (ATR at 47th percentile), regime mismatch, and model drift, identifying the true cause as low directional efficiency in 12h windows combined with a stale model. A retrain validated retroactively on the exact same calendar period returned +5.97% (winrate 67.07%). This kind of root-cause discipline distinguishes systematic research from performance rationalization.
  2. The edge is real, not noise. Randomized-label sanity check collapses predictive quality (F1 0.218 → 0.014) — the model is not memorizing randomness. Source: statistics/randomized_labels_report.csv.
  3. The edge is regime-dependent, not uniform. Strongest in bullish trend + high volatility + above 200DMA; weakest in bear rallies. Source: statistics/regime_report.csv, statistics/trend_regime_report.csv.
  4. Meta-regime filtering (BULL_AND_HIGHVOL) materially improves risk-adjusted quality: Sharpe 0.64 → 0.85, Max DD -14.4% → -7.4%, validated across 5 independent layers of evidence (walk-forward causal, sizing, Monte Carlo, SPA, retroactive 30-day test).
  5. A full 16-cell regime matrix (trend × volatility) was tested and rejected — on 1h candles, regime classification is unstable (median streak 3 hours), and on daily candles with proper smoothing it shows no discriminatory power beyond the unfiltered model. This is a negative result, documented rigorously, not omitted.
  6. Probabilities are not calibrated and should not be read as literal probabilities — they function as ranking/confidence heuristics. Isotonic/Platt recalibration improves Brier score but destroys F1 (0.269 → ~0.0000).
  7. Three production bugs were found and fixed in the live deployment (EV-gap scale error, LEVERAGE import, Telegram f-string), the first of which caused the bot to reject ~100% of valid trades for 20 days.

# Executive Summary {.unnumbered}

## What is S4

S4 is an EV-first, walk-forward, XGBoost-based systematic trading engine for **BTCUSDT Perpetual Futures** (1h timeframe). Direction is determined by predicted probability (`p_up`), position size by expected value and fractional Kelly, and trades are gated by an EV-gap threshold that requires the expected value to clear execution costs before a trade is taken.

## Current Classification

> **"Research-grade promising systematic strategy."**
> Not yet: institutional production system.
> Substantially beyond: hobby backtesting.

Source: `statistics/README.md`, Final Conclusions.

## Key Findings (validated, cross-checked)

1. **A live loss of -0.98% was diagnosed to root cause and reversed.** During 30 days of real paper trading (May–Jun 2026), the system lost -0.98% with a 39.66% winrate — far below the historically expected 74.22% for the same regime. A rigorous diagnosis ruled out low volatility (ATR at 47th percentile), regime mismatch, and model drift, identifying the true cause as low directional efficiency in 12h windows combined with a stale model. A retrain validated retroactively on the exact same calendar period returned +5.97% (winrate 67.07%). This kind of root-cause discipline distinguishes systematic research from performance rationalization.
2. **The edge is real, not noise.** Randomized-label sanity check collapses predictive quality (F1 0.218 → 0.014) — the model is not memorizing randomness. Source: `statistics/randomized_labels_report.csv`.
3. **The edge is regime-dependent, not uniform.** Strongest in bullish trend + high volatility + above 200DMA; weakest in bear rallies. Source: `statistics/regime_report.csv`, `statistics/trend_regime_report.csv`.
4. **Meta-regime filtering (BULL_AND_HIGHVOL) materially improves risk-adjusted quality**: Sharpe 0.64 → 0.85, Max DD -14.4% → -7.4%, validated across 5 independent layers of evidence (walk-forward causal, sizing, Monte Carlo, SPA, retroactive 30-day test).
5. **A full 16-cell regime matrix (trend × volatility) was tested and rejected** — on 1h candles, regime classification is unstable (median streak 3 hours), and on daily candles with proper smoothing it shows no discriminatory power beyond the unfiltered model. This is a negative result, documented rigorously, not omitted.
6. **Probabilities are not calibrated** and should not be read as literal probabilities — they function as ranking/confidence heuristics. Isotonic/Platt recalibration improves Brier score but destroys F1 (0.269 → ~0.0000).
7. **Three production bugs were found and fixed** in the live deployment (EV-gap scale error, LEVERAGE import, Telegram f-string), the first of which caused the bot to reject ~100% of valid trades for 20 days.
