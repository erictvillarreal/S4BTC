Executive Summary

What is S4

S4 is a systematic short-term trading engine for BTCUSDT Perpetual Futures, built on a structural mathematical edge in perpetual futures markets.

The core mechanism: with TP=2.0×ATR and SL=0.8×ATR, the geometric probability of a short winning in a random walk is 71.4%. BTC perpetual futures exhibit this property — price touches the short target before the long target in 73.5% of 1h candles historically. An EV-gap filter selects the highest-EV candidates each day. A walk-forward causal XGBoost model provides marginal directional guidance on top of that structural prior.

Simulated result (Oct 2024 – Jul 2026, 21 months): +325.7% vs. BTC hold +1.0%, MaxDD -0.60%, winrate 77.0%. Positive in all regimes tested including the $60k→$109k bull run.

Current Classification

“Research-grade promising systematic strategy.” Not yet: institutional production system. Substantially beyond: hobby backtesting.

Source: statistics/README.md, Final Conclusions.

Operational status (as of 28 Jul 2026): Paper trading on Railway — live and running. Last retrain: 25 Jul 2026 (retrain_pipeline.py, automated, equity $8,830, winrate 64.44%). Railway Volume (/app/persist, 500 MB) — state, model, and ledger persist across redeploys. Capital deployment decision pending: 27 Sep 2026 (3-month clean paper trading checkpoint).

Key Findings (validated, cross-checked)

  1. The edge is statistically real and survives adversarial testing. A randomized-label sanity check — the most direct test for model memorization — collapses F1 from 0.218 to 0.014 and recall to 0.009 when trade labels are shuffled. The model extracts genuine signal from price structure, not noise. SPA sign-flip p-value: 0.000 across all three market regimes (bear, lateral, bull) independently.

  2. The edge holds across market regimes — including bear markets. S4 generates 4.9x–5.5x the return of a buy-and-hold benchmark in bear, lateral, and bull conditions simultaneously, each at p=0.000. This was the key result that elevated S4 from a bull-market artifact to a structurally robust strategy.

  3. A live loss was diagnosed to root cause, not rationalized. During 30 days of live paper trading (May–Jun 2026), the system lost -0.98% with a 39.66% winrate — far below the historically expected 74.22% for the same regime. Three hypotheses were tested and rejected with data: low volatility (ATR at 47th percentile), regime mismatch, and model drift. The true cause: low directional efficiency in 12h windows (std 1.433% vs. historical 4.968% in the same regime) combined with a model never retrained past April 2026. A retrain on the exact same calendar period returned +5.97% (winrate 67.07%). The ability to distinguish root cause from noise is the difference between a research process and a rationalization process.

  4. Risk-adjusted quality improves materially with regime filtering. The BULL_AND_HIGHVOL filter — validated across 5 independent layers (walk-forward causal, position sizing, Monte Carlo, SPA, retroactive live test) — lifts Sharpe from 0.64 to 0.85 and halves maximum drawdown from -14.4% to -7.4%. The filter is implemented causally: no future data is used in the regime classification at any point.

  5. Negative results are documented, not omitted. A full 16-cell regime matrix (4 trend categories × 4 volatility quartiles) was designed, tested against held-out 2025–2026 data, and rejected: all 16 cells produced Sharpe ≈ 0.75 — statistically indistinguishable from the unfiltered baseline (Sharpe 0.7456). On 1h candles, regime streaks last a median of 3 hours. On daily candles with 14-day smoothing, streaks stabilize to weeks but lose discriminatory power. The 16-cell approach was archived, not deployed.

  6. Production parity with backtest required explicit enforcement. Three bugs were found in the live deployment. The most consequential: a 6,000x scale error in the EV-gap filter caused the bot to reject ~100% of valid trades for 20 days — silently, without crashing. The root cause was a divergence between walk.py (backtest) and s4_policy.py (production): the same logical filter implemented with different units. Discovering and fixing this gap is the reason a production parity audit exists as a distinct phase in this research.

  7. Regular retraining is the single most important operational variable. Simulated performance Oct 2024 – Jul 2026 (21 months): +325.7% with MaxDD -0.60% and 77.0% winrate, vs. BTC buy-and-hold +1.0% in the same period. Positive across all market regimes including the $60k→$109k bull run. The only confirmed underperformance (-0.98%, May–Jun 2026) was caused by a model not retrained for 60+ days. Regime filters tested exhaustively and rejected. The retrain cadence (every 14 days) is the mechanism.

  8. p_up is a ranking signal, not a calibrated probability. Isotonic Regression and Platt Scaling improve the Brier score but destroy F1 (0.269 → ~0.0000) by collapsing predictions toward the center. The model’s edge lives in the extremes of its score distribution. This limits Kelly-based sizing to conservative fractional variants and rules out probability-weighted portfolio construction until a calibration method that preserves the ranking structure is found.
