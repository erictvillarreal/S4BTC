6.3 Research Completed (Jul 2026 R&D Sessions)

The following gaps from the original audit have been investigated and resolved — findings documented in research/r-and-d-v1:

H1 — Momentum (REJECTED): No positive serial autocorrelation in 1h returns across any volatility regime. BTC does not exhibit momentum at 1h scale — mean-reversion dominates, especially in LOW_VOL.

H2 — Liquidity premium (NOT CONCLUSIVE): Volume-based liquidity does not predict win rate (p=0.559). Edge is session-dependent (Europe 08h UTC best) but not liquidity-dependent.

PnL Attribution (COMPLETE): Short trades = 100.3% of all PnL. Edge improves year-over-year (2022: Sharpe 0.73 → 2026: Sharpe 0.92). Model confidence (p_up) inversely correlated with win rate — confirmed p_up is ordinal ranking, not calibrated probability.

Regime Filters (ALL REJECTED): BULL_AND_HIGHVOL, 16-cell matrix, Kaufman Efficiency Ratio, MA200 daily — none improved net returns. The system performs best with NO regime filter and regular retraining.

Camino A — Symmetric labels (REJECTED): TP=SL=1.0×ATR system achieved WR=48.37% (below 50% breakeven), killed by kill-switch. The model has no directional predictive power without the structural prior of asymmetric TP/SL.

Core finding: S4’s edge is a structural mathematical advantage (TP=2.0×ATR, SL=0.8×ATR gives 71.4% geometric short prior) combined with EV-gap filtering. The XGBoost model adds marginal signal on top. Regular retraining (every 14 days) is the single most important operational variable — not regime filters.

Simulated performance Oct 2024 – Jul 2026: +325.7% vs BTC +1.0%, MaxDD -0.60%, WR 77.0%, across all market regimes.

6.4 Open Research Gaps

6.4.1 H3 — Funding Rate / Behavioral (PENDING DATA)

Hypothesis: S4 captures predictable behavior of over-leveraged retail longs in BTC perpetual futures, amplified during European session open (08h UTC) when institutional flow resolves Asian session imbalances. Blocked by: OKX API limits to ~3 months of funding rate history. Requires: Coinglass or CryptoQuant historical data (paid).

6.4.2 Kaufman Efficiency Ratio as Regime Signal (OPEN QUESTION)

ER past does not predict ER future (r=-0.023, p=0.209). ER future correlates with outcome but negatively (r=-0.309, p=0.000) — system wins MORE in choppy markets. Not actionable ex-ante. Pending: investigate if funding rate extreme predicts ER future.

6.4.3 Probabilistic Calibration (LOW PRIORITY)

p_up functions as ranking signal. Isotonic/Platt destroys F1. Requires stable final model before attempting calibration. Blocked by: model still evolving with retrains.

6.4.4 PnL Attribution — Remaining (QUICK WIN)

Kelly bucket and day-of-week attribution — ~30 min of work. FOMC calendar crosscheck — public dates, no cost.

6.4.5 Microstructure Features (MEDIUM PRIORITY)

OKX fetcher implemented, 697 rows generated. Features: oi_change_1h, oi_change_8h, oi_zscore_24h, volume_oi_ratio, funding_rate, funding_sign, funding_extreme. Pending: integrate into model retrain, reconstruct 2022-2026 history.

6.4.6 Meta-Regime Filter Deployment (DEPRIORITIZED)

Research showed BULL_AND_HIGHVOL improves Sharpe 0.64→0.73 causally, but regime filters rejected in R&D as net-negative on total returns. Revisit only if a specific regime-dependent hypothesis (H3) validates.
